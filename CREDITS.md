# Credits

## Assets

All of the simulator's visuals are generated in code. There are no downloaded 3D models,
textures, HDRIs or fonts.

- **Car**: a body lofted from cross-sections in `src/scene/car/body.ts`, modelled by eye after
  public photos of the 2026 Roadster that the project owner shared. It is built from scratch and
  carries no Tesla logos, wordmarks or badges; the number plate is blank.
- **Ramp, night sky, ground, lights and reflections**: shaders, simple geometry and in-scene light
  panels (`src/scene/environment/`).
- **Sound clips** (`public/audio/`): the motor loop (`motor.mp3`), the landing burst of air
  (`landing.mp3`) and the applause (`applause.mp3`) were generated by the project owner with
  ElevenLabs. They are not covered by this repository's MIT or CC BY 4.0 licences; their use is
  governed by ElevenLabs' terms for the plan they were made with. If you reuse the project,
  replace them or check those terms.
- **Other sound**: a soft hiss for the smaller nozzle bursts, synthesised with the Web Audio API
  (`src/audio/engine.ts`).
- **Fonts**: your system's UI font.
- **Diagrams in "How it works"**: hand-written SVG and CSS in `src/article/article.ts`.

If a future contribution adds an HDRI, texture or sound, it must be CC0 (e.g. from Poly Haven) and
be credited here.

## Facts and physics

Reporting on the reveal comes from the outlets listed in [`docs/SOURCES.md`](docs/SOURCES.md).
The physics follows standard textbook relations; NASA Glenn Research Center's educational pages
on isentropic flow, choking and rocket thrust are cited there too. CellCog's independent
estimates were used as a cross-check.
